Abstract

This paper introduces VMHQA, a Vietnamese multiple-choice question answering dataset for the mental health domain. The dataset contains 10,000 curated records across 1,166 subjects, including DSM-5 topics, and 8,599 supporting paragraphs from trusted medical and academic sources. Each item follows a USMLE-style format with question, options, answer, and evidence context. The paper benchmarks BERT-based models, 7B-9B LLMs, GPT frameworks, and Retrieval-Augmented Generation with Agentic Chunking, showing the value of structured retrieval for domain-specific mental health QA.
